# mushin.space

The Seonso movement is the home page; Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for all of Seonso's projects (Mushin, the familiars).
Published at https://mushin.space (privacy at /privacy/, terms at /terms/).

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The home: the Seonso movement page, served as written (no front matter, so no theme layout). Edit it here. |
| `seonsomovement/` | The movement page's files (styles, script, tokens, assets); its `index.html` forwards to `/`. |
| `seonso-movement/` | Forwards the oldest path to `/`. |
| `privacy.md`, `terms.md` | Legal pages for all Seonso projects. The home links them in its footer (one line: Privacy · Terms · contact) (Google's sign-in review needs that link). |
